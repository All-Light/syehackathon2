Marketing prizes

## A prize for posting about your build.

A real prize for posting publicly about the weekend. It is separate from every other prize, so you can win it and still place in your track.

- **Linkup marketing prize** 2 500 kr cash + 2 500 kr credits
- **Freebuff marketing prize** 2 000 kr cash

For a post to count, it has to do three things:

- Say what the tool is, worked in naturally so it does not read like an ad.
- Say how it actually helped your team during the build.
- End with a clear call to action, like "Check out Linkup" or "Try Freebuff".

Each prize requires its own separate post. You cannot mention both companies in the same post. Post on any platform, most people use LinkedIn, then add the link when you submit. The team with the most real distribution and attention wins each prize. [See all prizes.](https://sweyoung.com/hackathon/#prizes)

What you submit

## Exactly what goes in the form.

Everything is submitted once, through the [project submission form](https://sweyoung.com/submit/). Below is every single thing it asks for, in order. Nothing else is needed. Nothing is optional unless it literally says optional. After the deadline, the judges pick the top 10 submissions to pitch live on Sunday, so make it count.

1. **Project name, team name and team members.** The full names of everyone on your team.
2. **Which track or tracks you want to be judged in.** Tick every track that fits your project. Each track is scored on its own weighting, so ticking more can only help you.
3. **Demo video link.** A short video showing your product actually working. Use Loom, YouTube or Google Drive, and set the link so anyone can view it. If we cannot open it, we cannot judge it.
4. **GitHub link.** A link to your code. This is required and the judges open it. It's how we check the build is real and yours, so no one can cheat. No GitHub link, no prize.
5. **Live product link. Optional.** If people can use your product live, paste the link. If not, just leave it blank.
6. **Proof files.** Screenshots of your paying users, signed LOIs or customer history. If you are entering the Aqua Voice prize, include the app's downloaded history or screenshots of it too. Up to 8 files.
7. **What you built, and your traction, in writing.** What existed before the weekend, what you built during it, and the proof it's real. Be specific with numbers.

Everything you submit must be real and built by your team. Faking anything, including a friend paying for your own product, or submitting after the Sunday 10:00 deadline, means instant disqualification. The judges open your GitHub and demo and check.

FAQ

## The questions people actually ask.

How does pitching work?

After the deadline, the judges pick the top 10 submissions. Those 10 teams pitch live on Sunday: a 3 minute pitch and 2 minutes of questions, with a live demo. From those 10, the top 3 also pitch at Nordic Tech Week, and one team wins first place overall.

What should my pitch include?

Problem, solution, demo, traction, business potential and what you built during the weekend.

What do we submit?

See the full [What you submit](https://sweyoung.com/hackpack/#submit-checklist) list above. It walks through every field in order. In short: your project and team details, the tracks you want judged, a demo video link, your GitHub link (required), an optional live product link, and proof files showing real traction or your Aqua Voice history.

What gets a team disqualified?

Faking anything. If your "paying users" are really friends paying for your own product, that's fake and you're out. Faked traction, a staged demo, or code that isn't yours: out. And a submission after the Sunday 10:00 deadline is disqualified, no exceptions. Everything you submit has to be real and built by your team. The judges open your GitHub and demo and check.

How do the marketing prizes work?

There are two marketing prizes: one for Linkup and one for Freebuff. Post about the tool, say what it is without sounding like an ad, say how it helped your team, and end with a call to action. See [the marketing prizes](https://sweyoung.com/hackpack/#marketing-prize) for the full rules.

Can I switch tracks?

Yes, until your first submission.

Can I compete in more than one track?

Yes. Tick every track your project should be judged in when you submit. Each track is scored against its own weighting.

Do you cover travel?

If you are selected and coming from outside Sweden, yes. We cover travel from anywhere in Europe. Tell us where you are travelling from once you have your place and we will sort it out with you directly.

Where do I sleep?

At the venue, if you decide to sleep at all. Bring a sleeping bag or a blanket. If you would rather have a bed, you are free to book your own hotel.

Is food included?

Yes. Food, snacks and drinks are all covered, from Friday dinner to Sunday brunch.
