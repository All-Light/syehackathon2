Prizes

## Three kinds of prizes.

One big prize for the overall winner, category prizes for specific tracks and tools, and two separate marketing prizes just for posting about your build. They are separate, so you can win more than one.

First place

### 1st

For the whole team.

- **Cash prize** 25 000 kr
- **An interview with Entrepreneur First** guaranteed
- **3 years of Aqua Voice Max** 8 640 kr per member
- **3 months of ElevenLabs Pro** 2 970 kr per member
- **12 months of Linear Business** 1 920 kr per seat
- **2 months of office space at Sting** about 24 000 kr
- **One week of office access + coaching at SSE Business Lab** for the winning team
- **Prelint credits** 25 000 kr
- **Strawberry credits** 75 000 per member

The top 10 teams pitch to the judges. The top 3 of those pitch to 2,000 people at Nordic Tech Week.

Category

### Edtech B2C

The strongest project in the Edtech B2C track. Tick that track when you submit and you are in the running.

- **Prize pool** 10 000 kr

Category

### Best build with ElevenLabs

Open to every track. Won by the best product built with ElevenLabs voice AI. Use ElevenLabs and you are automatically considered.

- **ElevenLabs Scale** 8 970 kr per member

Category

### Best build with Linear

Open to every track. Won by the team that runs their build best on Linear. Use Linear and you are automatically considered.

- **Linear swag** to be confirmed

Category

### Aqua Voice cool project

Open to every track. Use Aqua Voice as part of your build. Aqua Voice picks the coolest project made with it.

- **Cash** US$500

Marketing prize

### Linkup marketing prize

Post publicly about your build and how Linkup helped, then add the link when you submit. Best real distribution wins. Full rules are in the hackpack.

- **Cash** 2 500 kr
- **Linkup credits** 2 500 kr

Marketing prize

### Freebuff marketing prize

Post publicly about your build and how Freebuff helped, then add the link when you submit. Best real distribution wins. Full rules are in the hackpack.

- **Cash** 2 000 kr

Tracks

01

### Outbuild

Build a real business and put it in front of real customers. Weighted hardest on traction and business potential, so paying users win here.

02

### AI Agents

Agents that do real work, not chat wrappers. Weighted on the problem and on whether an agent is genuinely the right tool to solve it.

03

### Wildcard

Anything goes. Weighted hardest on originality, for the surprising, memorable build that does not fit the other tracks.

04

### Edtech B2C

Consumer products that help people learn. Weighted on traction and on a real path to becoming an edtech company.
